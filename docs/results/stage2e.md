## Shipped variant, verdict and attribution

**Shipped: `rot_mode="dst"` with `table_mcast` on** (the `*_fk2dm` rows), now the default in
`PaluRuntime`, `build_model`, the three CLIs and the op. All figures here are `min_ms`, as in the tables above
and the S4 bars. Against the same-day `legacy` re-measurement (`*_fk2r`) the shipped rows
decode -8.9 % / -14.6 % / -18.1 % at 1k / 4k / 8k on `palu_full_50`, -8.8 % / -14.3 % / -17.6 % on
`palu_full_70`. Runner-up `pack` + multicast (`*_fk2pm`) is behind everywhere: same fixed term, 5.7 us
more per chunk.

**Verdict.**

- **S1 (correctness) -- met**, flags from the defaults, not the command line:
  `test_palu_sdpa_device.py` 93 passed at the S1 run (69afa00); 98 collected after the `rot_mode`
  parametrisation; the 11 multicast/default cases were re-run at 3f1c902 and all 98 passed. `test_attention_device.py -k "fused or glrd"`: 13 passed / 14 deselected. `decode_check`
  min PCC over 63 steps: 0.9948 / 0.9982 / 0.9979 on `palu_full_50_fk2dm`, `palu_full_70_fk2dm` and
  `glrd_50_fk2gdm` (group mode, H = 2), all above 0.99. G-LRD beat its same-dump reference
  `glrd_50_f256_fk2` (per-head kernel, `--group-heads 1`) by 9.7 % at 4k, about 1.6 points of it the
  H = 1 -> H = 2 step measured on `glrd_0.5` in stage 2c; p50/min <= 1.001.
- **S2 (fixed cost; must <= 78 us, stretch <= 65) -- met, both bars:** 118.7 -> 57.1 us per layer at
  `r_k` 32 / `r_v` 128.
- **S3 (per-chunk; must <= 101 us, stretch <= 95) -- met, both bars:** 114.4 -> 85.8 us per layer,
  2.7 us *below* stock's own 88.5.
- **S4 (whole step) -- must met, stretch missed:** 4k 65.0 -> 55.5 ms (bar 60.0), 1k 42.8 -> 39.0 (bar
  40.5), 8k 94.5 -> 77.4. Stretch was stock parity: 0.1 ms (0.2 %) short at 4k, 1.0 ms (2.7 %) at 1k.
- **S5 (bfp4) -- table delivered, recommendation withheld** (below).

**Attribution: both "idle resource" hypotheses held, orthogonally.** Against stock's 47.8 us fixed +
88.5 us per chunk, stage 2d's `legacy` carried 70.9 us of excess fixed cost, 25.9 us per chunk.

- *Table traffic was the fixed cost.* Multicast alone moves the fixed term 118.7 -> 57.4 us (-61.3,
  **86 % of the fixed excess**), per-chunk term untouched (114.4 -> 114.3).
- *Choreography was the per-chunk cost.* With the table still per core, `pack` moves the per-chunk term
  to 91.5 us (-22.9, **88 % of that excess**) and `dst` to 85.6 (-28.8, **111 %**, past stock),
  neither touching the fixed term (118.0, 118.6).
- Combined: 57.1 + 85.8 us -- **+9.3 us fixed, -2.7 us per chunk vs stock**.

**What the levers did not deliver.** No mode failed: all three clear the op bars at every calibrated
rank, and multicast is bit-identical to the per-core read on all five layouts. `pack` won
nothing extra -- `dst` dominates it at the same fixed cost, leaving the spec's optional chunk-level init
hoisting (+224 KB of L1) unexercised. Nor is the whole-step residual the prologue: in
`profile_{baseline,palu_full_50_fk2dm}_{1024,4096}.json` attention is +0.07 ms at 1k and **-0.87 ms at
4k** -- faster than stock -- while a context-independent **+1.1 ms of `layout` ops** (1.47 ms per
`palu_full_50` row vs stock's 0.38) carries the gap. The 9.3 us fixed term is <= 0.30 ms over 32
layers; layout, not half-tile Q math, is the next lever.

**bfp4 latents.** The bfp4 column above is mixed: `palu_full_50` is still prefill-blind, while
`palu_full_70`, re-measured under stage 2f's cache-faithful prefill, moved 9.028 -> **9.923** --
what blindness hid. For the blind row the evidence is decode-side PCC (0.9980 ->
0.9935 at ratio 0.5, 0.9988 -> 0.9959 at 0.7, both above 0.99), the bytes (573.8 -> 303.8
B/token/layer/chip at ratio 0.5; 760.8 -> 402.8 at 0.7, its bfp4 half from stage 2f) and unchanged
decode time (65.0 -> 64.7 ms at 4k). Stage 2f decides adoption.

**Caveats.**

- *Same-day noise.* `_fk2r` is `legacy` re-measured in the same quiet pass as the five new variants
  (bench cells re-run; profile artifacts renamed from a `_fk2` run, commit `accb4d9`), stage 2d's own `_fk2`
  files unchanged. It agrees with `_fk2` to 0.14 % on `min_ms` (worst cell: `palu_full_70` at 4k),
  to 0.01 us on the headline `r_k` 32 / `r_v` 128 fit and to at most 1.7 us (1.5 %) on the narrower
  signatures -- far inside the 9-18 % variant deltas.
- *Chip imbalance in the per-op profiles.* 17 of 36 variant profiles show a chip0/chip1
  device-time gap above 1 % (worst 16.0 %, `profile_palu_full_70_fk2p_1024.json`), all in `ccl` --
  attributed from the tracy csv at capture time, chip 1 kept only as a total in the JSONs. A CPU
  calibration queue was loading the host; a reduce-scatter absorbs dispatch skew as peer wait, so
  those rows' `ccl` and `device_ms_per_step` are untrustworthy; the attention ops feeding the fits are per-chip
  compute and internally consistent (0.00-0.05 % on clean runs), and the whole-step numbers come from the
  quiet bench pass. A <= 1 % gate should join the acceptance rules.
- *No attention-only bench.* The variant series come from the per-op profiles
  (`profile_attention_ctx_2e.png`); `bench --what attention` never ran; nothing cross-checks the
  fits host-side.
- *Double rounding.* Both new modes add `B sin` onto the L1-staged `A cos` in the packer, so `K_rot`
  rounds to bf16 twice. Op-level PCC against `legacy` at `r_k` 32 / 64 / 96 is
  0.9999968 / 0.9999962 / 0.9999973 (`pack`) and 0.9999913 / 0.9999904 / 0.9999935 (`dst`), far inside
  the 0.999 bar.
