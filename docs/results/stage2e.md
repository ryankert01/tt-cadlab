## Shipped variant, verdict and attribution

**Shipped: `rot_mode="dst"` with `table_mcast` on** (the `*_fk2dm` rows), now the default in
`PaluRuntime`, `build_model`, the three CLIs and the op. On `palu_full_50` it decodes in
39.0 / 55.5 / 77.4 ms at 1k / 4k / 8k against the same-day `legacy` re-measurement's 42.8 / 65.0 / 94.5
(-8.9 % / -14.6 % / -18.1 %); on `palu_full_70`, 39.3 / 56.9 / 80.5 against 43.1 / 66.5 / 97.7
(-8.8 % / -14.3 % / -17.6 %). Runner-up `pack` + multicast (`*_fk2pm`), 39.5 / 57.0 / 80.7, is behind at
every context: same fixed term, 5.9 us more per chunk.

**Verdict.**

- **S1 (correctness) -- met**, with the shipped flags coming from the defaults, not the command line:
  `test_palu_sdpa_device.py` 93 passed; `test_attention_device.py -k "fused or glrd"` 13 passed / 14
  deselected; `decode_check` min PCC over 63 steps 0.9948 / 0.9982 / 0.9979 for `palu_full_50_fk2dm`,
  `palu_full_70_fk2dm` and `glrd_50_fk2gdm` (group mode, H = 2 on all 32 layers), all above 0.99. The
  G-LRD bench beat its same-dump legacy reference: 51.3 / 95.6 / 154.7 ms against `glrd_50_f256_fk2`'s
  54.3 / 106.0 / 174.9 (-9.8 % at 4k), p50/min <= 1.001.
- **S2 (fixed cost; must <= 78 us, stretch <= 65) -- met, both bars:** 118.7 -> 57.1 us per layer at
  `r_k` 32 / `r_v` 128.
- **S3 (per-chunk cost; must <= 101 us, stretch <= 95) -- met, both bars:** 114.4 -> 85.8 us per layer,
  2.7 us *below* the stock op's own 88.5.
- **S4 (whole step) -- must met, stretch missed:** 4k 65.0 -> 55.5 ms (bar 60.0), 1k 42.8 -> 39.0 (bar
  40.5), 8k 94.5 -> 77.4 reported. The stretch was stock parity: 0.1 ms (0.2 %) short at 4k, 1.0 ms
  (2.7 %) short at 1k.
- **S5 (bfp4) -- table delivered, recommendation withheld:** PCC clears 0.99 on both dumps and the bytes
  halve, but the perplexity half of the criterion was not evaluable (below).

**Attribution: both "idle resource" hypotheses held, and they are orthogonal.** Against stock's 47.8 us
fixed + 88.5 us per chunk, stage 2d's `legacy` carried 70.9 us of excess fixed cost, 25.9 us per chunk.

- *Table traffic was the fixed cost.* Multicast alone moves the fixed term 118.7 -> 57.4 us: -61.3 us,
  **86 % of the fixed excess**, per-chunk term untouched (114.4 -> 114.3). The per-core DRAM read of the angle table was
  essentially the whole prologue.
- *Choreography was the per-chunk cost.* With the table still per core, `pack` moves the per-chunk term
  to 91.5 us (-22.9, **88 % of the per-chunk excess**) and `dst` to 85.6 (-28.8, **111 %** -- past
  stock), neither touching the fixed term (118.0, 118.6). The L1 round trip and the SFPU pass cost
  more than the excess they were blamed for.
- Combined: 57.1 + 85.8 us -- **+9.3 us fixed and -2.7 us per chunk against stock**.

**What the levers did not deliver.** No mode failed its tests: all three clear the op bars at every
calibrated rank, and multicast is bit-identical to the per-core read on all five layouts. What did not
arrive is a second win from `pack`: dominated by `dst` at the same fixed cost, so the spec's optional
chunk-level init hoisting for it (+224 KB of L1) was never worth implementing and stays unexercised. The
fixed term also stops 9.3 us per layer above stock -- what is left of the prologue is not the angle
table, and the next lever for it (half-tile Q math) was out of scope. That residual is why the 1k gap
survives, where the fixed terms dominate, and all but closes at 4k.

**bfp4 latents.** The perplexity column above is prefill-blind: the stage 2e instrument rebuilds
K/V from the bf16 latent and never sees the stored format, so its two columns agree to 15 digits by
construction and prove nothing. The real evidence is the decode-side PCC (0.9980 -> 0.9935 at
ratio 0.5, 0.9988 -> 0.9959 at 0.7, both above 0.99), the bytes (680 -> 360 B per token per layer per
chip) and an unchanged decode time (65.0 -> 64.7 ms at 4k); stage 2f's cache-faithful prefill
supersedes that column and decides adoption.

**Caveats.**

- *Same-day noise.* `_fk2r` is `legacy` re-measured in the same quiet pass as the five new variants
  (bench cells re-run; profile artifacts renamed from a `_fk2` run, commit `accb4d9`), stage 2d's own
  `_fk2` files unchanged. It agrees with `_fk2` to 0.03 % on `min_ms` and 0.01 us on both fitted terms
  -- far inside the 9-18 % variant deltas.
- *Chip imbalance in the per-op profiles.* 17 of the 36 variant profile JSONs show a chip0-vs-chip1
  device-time gap above 1 % (worst 16.0 %, `profile_palu_full_70_fk2p_1024.json`), all in the `ccl`
  category: these ran while a CPU calibration queue loaded the host, and a reduce-scatter's device time
  absorbs dispatch skew as peer wait. The attention-op numbers feeding the fits are per-chip compute and
  internally consistent (clean runs sit at 0.00-0.05 %; the fits reproduce), so the fits and the
  attention category are trustworthy while those rows' `ccl` category and `device_ms_per_step` are not.
  The whole-step numbers come from the quiet bench pass. A chip-imbalance gate of <= 1 % should join the
  acceptance rules for future profile runs.
- *No attention-only bench.* Section 1's series come from the per-op profiles
  (`profile_attention_ctx_2e.png`); `bench --what attention` was never run for the six variants, so no
  host-side attention timing cross-checks the fits.
- *Double rounding.* `pack` and `dst` write `A cos` to L1 and add `B sin` onto it in the packer, so
  `K_rot` rounds to bf16 twice, not once. Op-level PCC against `legacy` at `r_k`
  32 / 64 / 96 is 0.9999968 / 0.9999962 / 0.9999973 (`pack`) and 0.9999913 / 0.9999904 / 0.9999935
  (`dst`) -- `dst` the further of the two (its products come from fp32 DST matmul output, not a bf16 `AB`
  tile), both far inside the 0.999 bar.
