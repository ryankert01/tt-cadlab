# Stage 2g: group-shared mode at eight cores per item (2026-09-24)

Plan: `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-09-24-group-mode-eight-cores.md`. Numbers: `docs/results/stage2g_group_cap.md`
(generated by `scripts/sweep_group_cap_summary.py` from `docs/results/raw/{bench_attention,profile}_g8_*.json`).
Runners: `scripts/sweep_group_cap.sh` (wall-clock attention cells), `scripts/sweep_group_cap_profile.sh`
(device-profiled decode steps). Submodule 75d4765 (the three-round hang fix) or later is required; the cap is
lifted per run with `PALU_GROUP_MAX_CORES_PER_ITEM=8`, which the program factory and `palu_tt.fused._split_for`
both read.

## Question

With the three-round reduction hang fixed, does splitting a group-mode item over 8 cores (three tree
rounds, full grid at batch 4 and 8) beat the shipped four-core cap, and does any group layout now beat the
per-head kernel below batch 32, where the selector currently never chooses group mode?

## Method

Device profiles (tracy, three timed decode steps, 32 layers, context 4096) on the two shipped G-LRD dumps
(`compressed/glrd_0.5_f256`, `compressed/glrd_0.7_f256`) at batch 8, 4 and 1 with `--group-heads` forced to
1, 2 or 4 and the cap at 4 or 8. The attention category's device milliseconds per step is the deciding
number; whole-step device time is reported beside it. The wall-clock attention-only bench was run first
(34 cells) and is kept for the record only: at batch 16 and below its cells are 1 to 3 ms, mostly flagged
by the p50/min > 1.03 rule and not monotonic in context, with the server idle (load 1.0). It is host-bound
there and cannot resolve the differences in question.

## Results (attention op, device ms per step, ratio to per-head at the same batch)

| dump | batch | per-head H = 1 | best group at cap 4 | best group at cap 8 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| glrd 0.5 | 8 | **20.18** | H = 2: 23.04 (x1.14) | H = 4: 25.30 (x1.25) |
| glrd 0.5 | 4 | **13.84** | H = 2: 20.92 (x1.51) | H = 2: 16.54 (x1.20) |
| glrd 0.5 | 1 | **9.39** | H = 2: 19.38 (x2.06) | H = 2: 13.41 (x1.43) |
| glrd 0.7 | 8 | **24.63** | H = 2: 28.14 (x1.14) | H = 4: 28.16 (x1.14) |
| glrd 0.7 | 4 | **16.46** | H = 2: 24.99 (x1.52) | H = 2: 19.83 (x1.21) |
| glrd 0.7 | 1 | **10.68** | H = 2: 22.73 (x2.13) | H = 2: 15.43 (x1.44) |

Whole-step device time follows the same order; the best group layout is 6 to 10 % slower per step than
per-head at batch 8 and 4, and 10 to 12 % at batch 1.

On `glrd_0.7` the forced H = 4 cells ran as H = 2 on the profiled layers (the L1 model rejects H = 4 on the
wider layers and falls back), which is why the H = 4 and H = 2 rows at cap 8 coincide there.

## Findings

1. **Eight cores per item is a real improvement for group mode**: 21 to 30 % less attention time than
   the four-core cap at batch 4 and 1 (glrd 0.5: 20.9 to 16.5 ms at batch 4, 19.4 to 13.4 ms at batch 1),
   and 17 % at batch 8 for H = 4. The three-round tree is now correct and fits L1 on every layer of both
   dumps; no cell failed.
2. **It still does not reach the per-head kernel.** Per-head is 14 to 25 % faster at batch 8, about
   20 % at batch 4 and about 43 % at batch 1 on the attention op. The stage 2c conclusion stands: below
   batch 32, every cross-core merge in group mode costs more than the latent reads it saves.
3. **The selector's choice is unchanged.** `fused.group_heads_per_core` picks the per-head kernel below
   batch 32 and H = 2 with no reduction at batch 32; neither depends on the cap. Raising the cap changes
   no shipped number. It only makes explicit `--group-heads 2/4` runs at small batch faster.
4. **Phase 2 (whole-step wall-clock decode) was not run**: the profiles already give whole-step device
   time, and no group layout won.

## Recommendation (for the user to decide)

Raise the cap to 8 in the factory and in `_group_cap()` so a forced group layout gets the faster tree, keep
the selector rule as it is, and leave the report untouched: no reported cell moves. The three-round
correctness fix itself (submodule 75d4765) is already in.
